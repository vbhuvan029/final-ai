About this platform

About the AI HealthCare System

This project is a final-year engineering application designed to bridge the gap between initial symptom onset and professional medical consultation. By leveraging Machine Learning techniques (Random Forest), the system provides preliminary disease predictions based on user-selected symptoms.

Key features

Designed to support early health guidance

AI Prediction

Trained Random Forest model for high-accuracy predictions.

Doctor Recommendation

Directs patients to the correct specialist.

Hospital Suggestion

Locates nearby clinics or hospitals.

Medical History

Securely tracks your medical history and interactions.

Medical Disclaimer

This tool is for educational and informational purposes only. It is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician.
